About

A small dark-fantasy world

You play three characters trying to escape a crumbling place. A young girl, a knight, a wanderer. Their stories overlap.

Pixel art, traps, simple combat, some puzzles. That's most of it.

  • Short — a couple of hours per run
  • Keyboard or controller
  • Built solo, in evenings
⚔ Chapter II — The Hollow Halls
Features

Built for explorers

◆

Atmospheric pixel world

A broken nightmare painted pixel by pixel. Every room hides a story you can read between the bricks.

⚔

Three playable heroes

Knight, child, wanderer — each with a distinct kit and a story that only fully resolves on a second playthrough.

⌬

Roguelike runs

Daily seeds and weekly mutators reshuffle the dungeon. Climb the global leaderboards or chase your own ghosts.

✦

Puzzles & traps

Lure enemies into deadly contraptions. Master scythe, hookshot, and pickaxe to crack each chamber open.
